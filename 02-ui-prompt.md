## What to design
A single-page, modern, futuristic **hackathon event website** for a college-level internal hackathon ("KLE Society's P.C. Jabin Science College — Internal Hackathon 2026"). Design all sections listed below as one continuous scrolling page plus a sticky navbar.

## Theme & Mood
- **Light theme — not dark mode.** Base palette: warm **beige / off-white** backgrounds.
- Accent colors: **white, dark navy blue, and light/sky blue.**
- Overall mood: **modern, clean, futuristic, premium** — think "tech event landing page," not "college notice board." Generous white space, soft shadows, rounded cards, subtle gradients (beige → white, or white → light blue).
- Typography: a clean geometric sans-serif for headings (bold, slightly tight letter-spacing) paired with a simple readable sans-serif for body text. Clear size hierarchy (large hero heading, medium section headings, comfortable body text).
- Subtle **3D / futuristic visual elements**: abstract low-poly shapes, soft glowing orbs, thin wireframe grids, or floating geometric objects in light-blue/navy tones — used as background accents, especially in the hero, not overwhelming the content.
- Micro-interactions: buttons with soft hover-lift and glow, cards that gently rise on hover, smooth fade/slide-in as sections enter the viewport.

## Layout — section by section

**Navbar (sticky, glassmorphism)**
- Left: logo placeholder + event short name.
- Right: nav links — Home, Guidelines, Problem Statement, and a standout "Register" button (filled, navy blue, white text, pill-shaped).
- On scroll: navbar background becomes semi-transparent white with blur.
- Mobile: collapses to a hamburger icon opening a slide-down menu.

**Hero**
- Full viewport height.
- Large bold headline (event name), supporting subheadline (college name + tagline), two CTA buttons side by side: primary "Register Now" (filled navy) and secondary "View Problem Statements" (outline style).
- Background: soft beige-to-white gradient with a subtle animated 3D abstract shape (rotating wireframe polyhedron or particle field) in light blue, positioned off to one side so it doesn't compete with the text.

**Guidelines**
- Section heading "Guidelines" centered or left-aligned.
- Content as a **grid of cards** (3 columns desktop, 1 column mobile), each card: small icon, short title, 1–2 line description. Cards have soft rounded corners, light shadow, subtle hover-lift.

**Problem Statement**
- Centered, simpler layout: short paragraph explaining teams must pick from the official SIH list, then one large prominent button "View Problem Statements on SIH" (navy filled, arrow icon).
- Optional light decorative element (a subtle grid pattern or soft blue glow) behind this section for visual continuity with the hero.

**Registration**
- A distinct visually "elevated" section — maybe a card floating on a slightly different background tone (light blue tint) to make it feel like the main conversion point.
- Short instructions list (team size, deadline placeholder, contact) + one large CTA button "Register Your Team."

**Footer**
- Dark navy background (this is the one place a darker tone is appropriate, for contrast/grounding) with white/light-blue text.
- Columns: College info, Quick links (repeat of nav anchors), Contact placeholders, Social icon placeholders.
- Small logo row placeholders.
- Thin divider, then copyright line.

## Responsiveness
- Design for desktop (1440px), tablet (768px), and mobile (375px).
- On mobile: hero text stacks and shrinks, guideline cards go single-column, nav collapses to hamburger, 3D element either simplifies or fades to a static gradient for performance.

## What NOT to do
- No full dark theme.
- No stock "corporate blue gradient" cliché look — keep it distinctive with the beige base.
- No cluttered hero (keep it to headline + subheadline + 2 buttons + one visual element).
- Don't embed the Google Form or SIH page inside the design — both are represented as buttons/links only.
